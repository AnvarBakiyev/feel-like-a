Attendance and Revenue Report - September 2026
Date:         October 1, 2026
To:           Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo
From:         Chief Transformation Officer
Wards:        All


SUMMARY

This report will update the Board of Management on the visitor statistics and revenue
results for the period 2026-01-01 through 2026-09-30. This report includes a dashboard
highlighting some of the key metrics for 2026.


RECOMMENDATIONS

The Chief Transformation Officer recommends that:

1. The Board of Management of the Toronto Zoo receive this report for information.


FINANCIAL IMPACT

There is no financial impact resulting from the adoption of the recommendations in this
report.


DECISION HISTORY

In accordance with Zoo By-law No. 2-2010, attendance and revenues are reported to
the Board on a regular basis. Budgeted targets account for any shifts in the calendar for
statutory holidays and number of weekends each month.

For your information, the results for attendance and the primary revenue categories on
a year-to-date basis are outlined in the tables and charts included in Attachment 1
(attached).

COMMENTS

Overall guest visitation through end of September 2026 was 1,095,286, falling short of
budgeted attendance by 105,314 guests (-9%). While attendance has continued to lag
budget as a result of weather impacts experienced through the first half of the year, this
is an improvement over July year-to-date attendance which was -14% below target,
attributable in part to August achieving 95% of budgeted attendance (227,036 guests)
and September exceeding target at 113% (161,435 guests). September's strong
attendance was buoyed by the launch of the Glow in the Wild evening lantern festival
which attracted more than 21,000 guests during its first month of operations. As a result,
year-to-date attendance has nearly fully recovered compared to 2025 visitation through
end of September.

Through end of September 2026, general admissions accounted for 48% of the overall
year-to-date attendance mix (compared to a budget target of 49%), with membership
accounting for 30% and the remaining 22% made up of school groups and non-paying
guests.

Similar to attendance patterns for September, revenue performance has demonstrated
some recovery relative to budget over the course of the past two month. At the end of
July, revenue performance had fallen to -9% compared to budget as a result of poor
attendance totals during the month. By end of September, revenue is tracking -6% off
budget target and 4% ahead of 2025 actuals. The impact of lagging visitation has been
softened by strong spend per guest performance. The average year-to-date revenue
per guest at $51.61 exceeds both budget and prior year actuals of $50.23 (3%) and
$49.17 (5%), respectively.

Admission sales revenue at -11% compared to budget and -2% compared to 2025
actual continues to lag given lower than forecast attendance. Year-to-date revenue
shortfalls through end of September have also been experienced in parking at -6%,
retail at -6%, rides and rentals at -8% and food services at -3%. Each of these
categories has seen improved performance since end of July as a result of improved
attendance through August and September in addition to the launch of Glow in the Wild.
This recovery has been further supported through continued higher than budgeted
revenue performance in education programs (4%) and membership (1%).

Attendance and revenues continue to be closely monitored with expenditure controls
implemented throughout the year to mitigate budgetary impacts.

Throughout August and September, the Zoo hosted several events to recognize, attract
and entertain guests and community members. This includes an Enchanted Celebration
on August 8th featuring meet and greets with fairy tale characters, Zoo After Dark: An
Evening with Native Bats event hosted August 29th, Brew at the Zoo hosted on
September 5th, the Oasis Zoo Run which took place on September 12th, and GenWell
Weekend hosted on September 19th and 20th aiming to bring people together to foster
community, connection and collaboration. Your Toronto Zoo also welcomed our
partners from Native Child and Family Services to recognize the National Day for Truth
and Reconciliation on September 30th with a day of programming and storytelling at the
